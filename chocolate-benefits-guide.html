CHOCOLATE BENEFITS

Understanding Dark Chocolate and Its Benefits

Dark chocolate contains cocoa compounds called flavanols. The amount can vary depending on the cocoa content and how the chocolate is processed.

What Are Cocoa Flavanols?

Flavanols are naturally occurring compounds found in cocoa. Their levels can differ between chocolate products because of ingredients and processing methods.

Why Does Cocoa Content Matter?

The percentage of cocoa can influence the flavour and composition of dark chocolate. A higher cocoa percentage often gives a more intense cocoa taste.

Enjoy Chocolate in Moderation

Chocolate can be enjoyed as part of a balanced diet. Portion size and the overall ingredients are worth considering when choosing a chocolate treat.
