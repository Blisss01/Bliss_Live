Dark Chocolate vs Milk Chocolate: What's the Difference?

Dark chocolate generally contains a higher percentage of cocoa solids and has a richer, more intense flavour. Milk chocolate includes milk ingredients, creating a creamier and sweeter taste.

What Is Dark Chocolate?

Dark chocolate is known for its more intense cocoa flavour. Its taste can vary depending on the percentage of cocoa solids and the recipe.

What Is Milk Chocolate?

Milk chocolate includes milk ingredients, which create a creamier texture and sweeter flavour.

Which One Should You Choose?

Both have their own unique appeal. Your choice can depend on your personal taste and preferred level of sweetness.
